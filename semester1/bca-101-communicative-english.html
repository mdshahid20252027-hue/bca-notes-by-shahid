1. Business Correspondence

Business correspondence refers to formal written communication used in business and professional situations.

📄 Structure of a Letter

  • Sender's Address
  • Date
  • Receiver's Address
  • Subject
  • Salutation
  • Body of the Letter
  • Closing
  • Signature

🔍 Inquiry Letter

A letter written to request information about a product, service, price, availability or other business matter.

🛒 Sales Letter

A formal letter written to promote or sell a product or service to a potential customer.

📦 Order Letter

A letter used to place an order for products or services.

⚠️ Complaints

A complaint letter communicates a problem or dissatisfaction with a product, service or transaction.

🤝 Complaint Handling

Complaint handling involves responding professionally, understanding the issue and providing an appropriate solution.

✉️ Routine Letter

Routine correspondence is used for regular, day-to-day professional communication.

🏛️

2. Government Correspondence

📑 Memo

A memorandum is a formal written communication used within an organization or institution.

📅 Agenda

An agenda is a list of items or topics planned for discussion during a meeting.

📝 Minutes

Minutes are the official written record of the proceedings and decisions of a meeting.

💡 Proposals

A proposal is a formal plan or suggestion presented for consideration or approval.

✍️

3. Writing Skills

📊 Report Writing

A report presents information about a specific subject in a structured and formal manner.

⚖️ Argumentative Composition

Writing that presents an argument or viewpoint supported by reasons and evidence.

🔎 Explanatory Composition

Writing that explains an idea, process or subject clearly.

🎨 Descriptive Composition

Writing that describes a person, place, object, event or situation.

📖 Narrative Composition

Writing that presents events in the form of a story or sequence.

📝 Paragraph Writing

A paragraph develops one main idea using logically connected sentences.

📘

4. Grammar

🔤 Sentence Structure

Learn how words and phrases are organized to form meaningful sentences.

💬 Idiomatic Usage

Understanding commonly used expressions whose meanings may differ from the literal meaning of individual words.

⏰ Tenses

Tenses indicate the time of an action or state.

🔄 Direct & Indirect Speech

Direct speech reports exact words, while indirect speech reports the meaning of what someone said.

🔡 Parts of Speech

Important categories include noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction and interjection.

🔁 Active & Passive Voice

Active voice focuses on the doer of an action, while passive voice focuses on the receiver of the action.

📚 Vocabulary

Development of word knowledge and appropriate word usage for effective communication.
